Let $G = \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}$, which we will denote $G = \mathbb{Z}^2$. Consider the subgroup $H = \langle(1,2)\rangle$.

  1. Plot at least four elements of $H$ as points in the $xy$-plane. Make sure that $(1,2)$ is one of the points that you plot.
  2. Choose a coset $(x_1, y_1) + H \neq H$, and plot at least four elements of this coset.
  3. Choose another coset $(x_2, y_2) + H \neq H$, and plot at least four elements of this coset.
  4. Qualitatively describe the geometry of these cosets when plotted.
  5. Prove that $H \triangleleft G$.
  6. Prove that $G/H \cong \mathbb{Z}$ by describing an explicit isomorphism $\varphi$.
