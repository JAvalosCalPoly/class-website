Given two matrices $A, B \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with entries $\{a_{ij}\}$ and $\{b_{ij}\}$ respectively, we define matrix multiplication as $AB = C \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ such that \( c_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^n a_{ik}b_{kj} \). Use this definition to prove that matrix multiplication distributes over matrix addition.
