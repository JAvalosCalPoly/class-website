Let \( (G, +) \) be an abelian group, and denote \[ \operatorname{Endo}(G) = \{\phi\colon G \to G \mid \phi \text{ is a homomorphism}\}. \] Prove that $\operatorname{Endo}(G)$, called the endomorphism ring, is a ring with under \(\boxplus\) and \(\boxtimes\), where \(\boxplus\) is pointwise addition and \(\boxtimes\) is function composition: \[ \begin{alignat*}{2} (\phi_1 \boxplus \phi_2)(g) &= (\phi_1 + \phi_2)(g) &&= \phi_1(g) + \phi_2(g) \\ (\phi_1 \boxtimes \phi_2)(g) &= (\phi_1 \circ \phi_2)(g) &&= \phi_1(\phi_2(g)). \end{alignat*} \]
